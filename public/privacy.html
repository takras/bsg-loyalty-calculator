Privacy Policy

BSG Companion · Effective 21 October 2026

BSG Companion is an unofficial, fan-made helper for Battlestar Galactica: The Board Game, available as a website at bsg.takras.net and as apps for Android and iOS. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: the app does not collect, share or sell any personal data.

What the app stores

To keep your game when you close the app or reload the page, BSG Companion saves your current setup on your own device: chosen expansions and options, the characters in play, title holders, player status and the game log, plus your theme choice. This information:

  • stays on your device (the browser's local storage, or the app's private storage on Android/iOS);
  • is never sent to us or to anyone else;
  • contains no names, contact details or other personal information – only game characters and options.

You can delete it at any time with the Reset button in the app, by clearing the site's data in your browser, or by uninstalling the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.
  • No network requests of its own: all content, images and fonts are included in the app.

The Android app declares the standard Internet permission that its built-in web view uses, but the app itself does not send or receive data. Links to other websites (for example the rulebook sources in the credits) open only when you tap them; those sites have their own privacy policies.

The website

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub receives standard technical information such as your IP address, as it does for any website, and handles it under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. BSG Companion itself does not receive this information.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
