BSG Companion – Support

An unofficial, fan-made helper for Battlestar Galactica: The Board Game.

Getting started

  • Setup: choose your expansions, ending and variants, then enter the characters your table picked, in seat order. The app checks the line-up and shows the exact Loyalty deck and setup steps.
  • Titles: after Start game, mark players as in the Brig or revealed Cylons – the President, Admiral and CAG titles pass down the lines of succession automatically.
  • Combat, Crisis, Execute, Rules: quick references for die rolls, Crisis activation icons, the execution procedure, common rules and the full searchable rulebook – all filtered to your setup.

Frequently asked questions

Does it need an internet connection?
The apps work fully offline. The website needs a connection to load.

Where is my game saved?
Only on your device. It is kept when you close the app; nothing is sent anywhere.

How do I start a new game?
Tap Reset at the top. This clears expansions, characters, titles and the log.

A rule looks wrong.
Please let us know (see below) with the expansions you use and the screen it is on. Rules are summarised from the official rules, FAQ and community rule compilations.

Is this an official app?
No. It is a free fan project and is not affiliated with Fantasy Flight Games, Universal or NBCUniversal. You need the board game to play.
